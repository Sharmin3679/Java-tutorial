2.1. Why are the variables declared as private?
Variables are declared as private to enforce encapsulation (data hiding). This prevents unauthorized direct access or accidental modification of student data from outside the class.

2.2. What happens if the variables are declared public?
If declared public, external classes can directly modify field values without any validation (e.g., setting a negative CGPA like -5.0 or altering student IDs directly), compromising data integrity and security.

2.3. Why do we use getters and setters?
Getters and setters provide controlled access to private variables. Setters allow us to validate data before updating variable values, and getters allow read-only access to internal fields safely.
